Fixed camera behind the penalty spot looking straight at the center of the far goal line, slight elevated classic arcade penalty camera, NOT isometric, NOT cinematic side view. At NIGHT, deep navy sky at top, packed spectator grandstand across top 30 percent, detailed tiny pixel crowd with colorful shirts, stadium structure and powerful cool-white floodlight towers at far left and far right, subtle dithered floodlight glow. Lower 70 percent is beautiful vibrant emerald grass with alternating mowing stripes receding horizontally into distance, rich clustered pixel shading, white penalty box lines in perspective. The goal line is approximately 43 percent down from the top; the penalty spot at center approximately 77 percent down. IMPORTANT: no goal frame, no net, no goalkeeper, no football, no foreground players: these will be separate sprites added by Unity. Keep the central field empty. Strong symmetric straight-on composition like classic browser penalty shootout arcade games, but polished original 16-bit pixel-art, visible square pixel clusters, crisp nearest-neighbor edges, no smooth painted brushstrokes. The art should feel playable and bright under stadium lights, not a dark silhouette. No logos, no brand signage, no readable letters, no UI, no watermark. This background is one scene layer, not a screenshot/mockup. Keep whole image full bleed with no border.
